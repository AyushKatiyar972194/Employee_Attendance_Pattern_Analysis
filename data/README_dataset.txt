# Canonical Dataset — Employee Attendance Pattern Analysis (EAPA-01)

Generated by `src/generate_data.py` with random seed 101.

- Five base relational tables match the SQLite schema with enforced foreign keys.
- 120 synthetic employees across 8 business divisions (Engineering, HR, Finance, Sales, Customer Support, Product Management, Quality Assurance, Operations).
- 248 department-calendar rows covering the full 31 calendar days of October 2026 (including multi-shift schedules, weekly offs, and public holidays).
- 2,400 attendance fact rows across all 20 working dates in October 2026.
- `vw_attendance_analysis` is a 34-column unified analytical view.
- 51 missing check-out timestamps (~2.3% of present records), 3 rejected duplicate candidates, and documented extreme delay and duration outliers are catalogued for data-quality auditing.
